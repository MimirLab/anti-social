# Platform icon artwork

All 21 platform marks are bundled Android vectors. anti social applies a monochrome
theme tint at runtime: soft light ink on dark surfaces and dark ink on light
surfaces. Large marks use a restrained rounded tile and optical sizing. Small
comment/attribution marks omit the tile. No icon service is contacted.

These marks identify third-party services and do not imply endorsement. Brand
names and trademarks remain with their respective owners. The locally drawn
Truth Social and Gab interpretations are unofficial.

## Simple Icons

The existing X, Bluesky, Reddit, Tumblr, TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest, Threads,
Facebook, Mastodon, Lemmy, Pixelfed, Misskey, Telegram and Snapchat paths come
from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/4d29a35c954699b389a834aacc97dd6e7e74143c/icons),
revision `4d29a35c954699b389a834aacc97dd6e7e74143c`, by Simple Icons contributors.
LinkedIn comes from the same project's
[7.0.0 release](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/7.0.0/icons/linkedin.svg).
The package is CC0-1.0; see `simple-icons-LICENSE.txt` and
`simple-icons-DISCLAIMER.txt`, including the distinction between the package
license and brand trademarks. Modifications: conversion from SVG paths into
Android vectors, optical sizing and local monochrome tint.

## anti social original interpretations

`platform_truthsocial.xml` (geometric T and detached terminal) and
`platform_gab.xml` (rounded conversation-g), and `platform_rss.xml` (geometric feed arcs) are locally drawn vector
interpretations under GPL-3.0-or-later, as with anti social’s source. They are
recognizable compact identifiers rather than text-rendered fallbacks, and are
not official artwork supplied by those services. See `LICENSE.GPL-3.txt`.
