anti social public pathway research and native adapters — 2026-10-17

anti social is GPL-3.0-or-later. Darwin remains the rebuilt routing donor; existing donor
assets, provenance hashes and GPL notices remain intact. New Kotlin data readers
are independently implemented contracts. No third-party server runtime, remote
JavaScript, account sessions, API secrets or executable JSON-LD contexts are bundled.

The complete platform-by-platform primary source atlas is bundled as
app/src/main/assets/source-discovery.json. Runtime candidates are separately
reviewed in public-pathways.json. Source-list inclusion is not proof of health.
