target-matched DFlash2 drafter. Preserve the target's ternary weight packing and

named tensors were found. PrismML explicitly documents the plain 4B and 9B
packs as the targets for their published DFlash2 files:

- [4B model and speculative instructions](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-4B-gguf#speculative-decoding),
  observed revision `8d6557168168f91ea6697a4a49ed2ef622b53adb`.
- [9B model and speculative instructions](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-9B-gguf#speculative-decoding),

- [2B model](https://huggingface.co/prism-ml/Ternary-Bonsai-2-2B-gguf#speculative-decoding)
  ships without a drafter; the authors report slower everyday decoding when
  testing a draft head at that size.

1,574,233,856 bytes. The MTP variants add 128,177,056 / 258,558,880 bytes to

native core contains DFlash2 and MTP implementations, but the Android text
bridge does not yet orchestrate speculation. Before admission, verify exact
target/drafter/tokenizer identity, output constraints, captured-feature memory,
rejection rollback of recurrent and protected-cache state, cancellation, reset,
and end-to-end Context latency against plain decoding. Draft weights/cache
quantization needs its own acceptance and resource measurements. Do not change
the target weights or assume another model's drafter is interchangeable.
