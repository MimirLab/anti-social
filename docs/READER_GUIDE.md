# Reading with anti social

## Start reading

Choose the social sites you want in Settings, then add public accounts or ordinary
RSS/Atom feeds in Subscriptions. Channels can combine sources with explicit rules.
Your accepted feed remains chronological. Open a shared link or search from a
site's icon; the app chooses a public route behind the scenes.

On a profile, Subscribe locally keeps the opened account ready to save while
other account suggestions arrive. Choose the additional accounts yourself;
Find more accounts checks matching names if the linked sources missed one.
Linked accounts also help fill missing readable names in posts. An account's own
published name takes priority. See [account discovery](ACCOUNT_LINKAGE.md) for
sources, limits and caching.

Use Continue reading to resume, or Jump to newest to skip missed material.
The visible card keeps its reading position while completed updates join the
deck. Returning from Search restores All or the last source used in Following.
Facebook is included in the default source selection; your custom selection stays
under your control in Settings.

Pull down at the newest edge to check subscriptions. A pull arrow becomes a
prominent progress bubble while the check runs. Feed options includes Stop
refresh and the per-account check details.

A finished batch gets one stable exit line encouraging you to leave the feed.
Caught up means the relevant refresh succeeded. Fetched batch finished describes
the loaded material, with unavailable sources still visibly pending. Skipped
unread posts and incoming updates retain their own status.

Collected cross-posts expose their original editions. Pictures and videos in a
multi-platform collection show their source platforms; a deduplicated picture
retains the platforms of its observed copies. Tap that label to inspect the
originals. Different pictures remain separate. In comments, All combines
available streams, while platform tabs let you inspect each stream. Community
participation belongs to All or its community tab; the app does not post into an
unconnected social account.

When corroborated copies have small wording edits, common text can appear once
with bracketed alternatives. The small colored platform marks identify each
fragment and open its original. Shared text stays neutral; full source editions
and discussions remain available. This is literal text alignment, without an AI
rewrite or a judgment about why a publisher changed its wording.

Use the Share icon at the bottom of any post to prepare its clean link and open
Android's sharing menu. The icon shows progress while the existing link checker
works. Tap a platform icon for that platform's original: share, copy its clean
link, open it, or read its comments. Discussions still opens the combined All
comment stack. Collected originals also have their own Share action.

## Choose the connection

Open **Settings → Privacy & connection → Tor for all traffic** to route the app's
content, photos, videos and updates through its embedded Tor connection. Requests
pause if that connection is unavailable. Commercial public-index searches and
regional discovery use Tor even when ordinary reading connects directly. The
status card distinguishes these modes. No root or separate Tor app is needed.

Network protection, URL cleaning and safe extraction apply in both modes. The
toggle controls this app; an explicit handoff to another app uses that app's
connection. See [the architecture](ARCHITECTURE.md) for the technical path.

## Find related accounts and communities

Tap the compass beside Subscribe locally on a public profile, or choose Discover
related in a subscription's menu. Initial connections appear while other sources
work. Filter with platform icons, search the collected cards with the magnifier,
and tap a connection reason to see how it was found. Use the person-plus to
subscribe. Explore further reads another bounded block; closing stops discovery.

Linked accounts can bridge into another platform's public graph. This remains
separate from finding the same person's accounts: a mention, follow or shared
commenter is a connection, not an identity claim. Sources shows coverage and
unavailable routes. See [Discover related](ADJACENT_DISCOVERY.md).

## Find a business or event

Open Search, select Businesses or Events, and choose a city and radius. The area
stays on your device; continuous GPS tracking is unnecessary. Miles/kilometres can
be changed in the area controls.

Search a name, service or activity. Initial cards arrive before supplemental
details finish. Tap a business for its published contacts, website, social
accounts and available branch menu. Phone/email actions use the relevant device
app. Menus retain dine-in/pickup/delivery distinctions when published.

Known chains share one card even when their initial directory records lack a
website. Open the location picker for each branch's address, phone and other
distinct details, ordered by distance from your selected area. Country, local
brand exceptions and compatible categories constrain the offline name lookup.

For events, choose a date window or use a recognized phrase such as “this weekend.”
“Something outdoors for toddlers” can match a children's nature walk; an unstated
age range remains marked. Expand Possible events only if you want to inspect
announcements with missing or uncertain details. Source evidence is inside the
detail sheet. Settings → Search → Read event flyers controls local image reading.

## Keep useful material

Save a local copy to preserve available text, attribution and selected media or
thread material. Collections organize saved material. Library search covers saved
copies and your chosen retained history. Ordinary cache expires independently;
saved material and explicit offline downloads are protected.

Settings → Storage → Clear cache removes disposable copies, while keeping
subscriptions, configuration, read progress and saved material. Refresh can
retrieve those same posts again. Clearing cache also repairs the empty-feed
suppression left by a manual purge in earlier releases; a reinstall is unnecessary.
An undecodable ordinary image copy is discarded rather than reused indefinitely.
Retry, or refresh after the connection recovers, to fetch it again. Successful
visible images are not fetched again just because a refresh finishes.

Export reader state from Settings for your own backup or another device. Share a
source/channel pack when you only want to share subscriptions. Recipients preview
and choose sources; packs do not silently install your personal reading history.

## Behind this post

The post's Info icon opens **Behind this post**. It shows available findings
immediately and checks generation recipes and linked creation credentials as you
read. Recipe details and verification explanations are expandable. Reopening
reuses completed checks. In saved copies, inspection stays offline. Info works
with AI disabled; see [Media Info](MEDIA_INFO.md) for supported formats and limits.

## Optional Context

Enable AI in Settings and select local models, your server or your own cloud
provider. Context appears only after AI is enabled. One press opens the cached
brief or starts generation. Read-ahead and saved-item refresh are separate
preferences; neither is required for normal reading.

Context aims to condense concrete useful information, including substantive
corrections found among noisy replies. It can miss details. Source evidence stays
available, and an unavailable article or thread is not silently replaced with
invented information. A noise-only update should not rewrite a useful cached brief.

## Card activity and Library discoveries

Updating… means the card has background work underway. Ready means there are no
current updates to wait for. Tap the status to see what is being added; you can
keep reading or move on while it works.

Saved businesses and events now live in Library. Use Businesses or Events, or
search across All. Opening a saved card reads its local snapshot; following a
source or social link is a separate action.
